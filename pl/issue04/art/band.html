Undiscovered Band of the Month

Written By Chi Chi

- Breedlove -

Breedlove is a five piece band out of Austin, Texas, featuring Dan Dyer on guitar and lead vocals, Tyrone Fullerton on lead guitar, Josh Dawkins on bass, Jason White on drums, and Ezra Reynolds on keyboards. They play a kind of soul influenced blues-rock that could only come from an outstanding city such as Austin. I've seen them twice now, both times at the Caravan of Dreams opening for my favorite band, Storyville, with about a two year gap in between the shows. When I first saw them, I thought they were a decent blues-rock band with a good singer, but didn't think they were anything outstanding. Either my memory is bad, or they improved greatly over this time period. Dan's voice was more beautiful and soulful than I remembered it being, and the band as a whole had become much better players with a much more mature sound and better understanding of soul and blues music. After rediscovering this band myself, I was disappointed to learn that they were breaking up. Despite this letdown, I'm sure that these young musicians will be making great music for years to come.
