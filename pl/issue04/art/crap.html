Crappy Band of the Month

Written By Chi Chi

This band was only recently added to my list, so they were the first on my mind.

That's right, this month's Crappy Band of the Month is:

- Tendryl -



They are a hardcore band in the D/FW area that I saw play at CDX a couple weeks ago, but did not enjoy. First, the bass player scared the hell out of me. HE was the freakiest gimp-looking hardcore guy, and it felt as if he was constantly watching me. I think he liked me. The guitar player tried to solo several times, only to fall short of what we in the music industry call good. The drummer wasn't absolutely horrible, but wasn't good enough to carry this untalented band. My assessment: not worth it.
