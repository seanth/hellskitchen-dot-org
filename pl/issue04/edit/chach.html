Ask Cha-Chee

Ask Cha-Chee anything you like about science, life, and the world we live in



Dear Cha-Chee,
How come I can only see one side of the moon?
-Nathan


 -Well,the answer to this question is very simple geometry. At any given time only one hundred and eighty degrees of any sphere may be seen from one viewpoint. You have only one viewpoint, namely, your head, so you can only see one side of the moon. Perhaps if the moon was translucent, you could see more.
-Cha-Chee




Dear Cha-Chee,
How does a toaster work, and why will it electrocute me if I try to use it in the bathtub?
-Jenna


 -A toaster works by heating up the bread you put inside of it until it is "Toasted." As for electrocuting you, don't be silly. Toasters use heat to toast your bread, and heat can't electrocute you. Use it in the bathtub all you like. Just please be very careful not to get your bread soggy. Nobody likes soggy bread.
-Cha-Chee

Dear Cha-Chee,
I heard that lemmings commit mass suicide by running off cliffs into the ocean, but it doesn't seem right to me for a species to be suicidal. Are they really suicidal or not?
-Wondering


 -Well, Wondering, we used to believe that this species was suicidal, until the "suicides" were more closely looked into. We came to find that all the dead lemmings were the stronger male lemmings, and after this mass suicide, the massive lemming colonies were dominated by a few small lemming males who had all the women lemmings whenever they wanted and made their children get them food whenever they were hungry. Every so often, the colonies of lemmings become overcrowded with strong males that compete with the smaller but much smarter lemmings for food and women lemmings. The smarter lemmings then claim to have built an invisible bridge that leads to the greatest hunting and mating grounds in the world, and all the stronger males rush to this wonderful place, only to find out that the invisible bridge has a hole in it right near the beginning.
-Cha-Chee
