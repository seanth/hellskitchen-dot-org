Ask Bishop Prevalance

Bishop Prevelance answers all of your religous questions



Dear Bishop,
I just watched all eight of the Kid's movies "All Dogs go to Heaven" (the original movie and its seven sequels). Do all dogs REALLY go to heaven, or are those movies just full of crap?
-Curious


 -Well, recent studies show that dogs rank very high on the "holy holy" scale (our new and improved chain of being), which would mean that even if all dogs do not go to heaven, enough do go to heaven to make that a safe approximation. We can be pretty sure, however, that they do not smell bad in heaven, and most everyone in heaven will probably want a dog anyway, so it all works out pretty nicely.
-Bishop Prevalance




Dear Bishop,
I also watched this movie where a guy died but he loved this girl so much that he came back from the dead and made pottery with her but she couldn't really see him until the end of the movie and I think eventually he went to hell for having sex after he was dead or something. Can that really happen?
-Curious (again)


 -Well, I will have you know that the Church has a pretty sweet deal going on with the film industry right now. I can assure you that anything you see on the screen or on your TV at home is 100% Christian-safe, and as accurate as possible while keeping the film industry and the church industry financially well off. So watch all you want, and believe every word, my friend.
-Bishop Prevalance




Dear Bishop,
I am an athiest, and many of my church-going friends avoid me and tell me I am wicked. I am really a nice guy though, and I would just like to have my friends back. Is there any way I can make them believe that I am a nice guy?
-Jerry


 -No, there isn't. It says in the bible that athiests will "follow the path to wickedness." You are going to hell, my friend, and although that may not scare you, it sure scares the hell out of me, so please do not send me any more of your questions and please leave the good Christian folk alone.
-Bishop Prevalance




Dear Bishop,
