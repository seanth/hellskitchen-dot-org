Starr to King: "Reno is Hiding the Protruding Truth."

-Written by Saul Rosenrosen

Washington DC-

In an effort to return credibility to his office, independent council Kent Starr has launched a multi-million dollar investigation into the gender of Attorney General Janet Reno.

"The Clinton-Lewinsky debacle hurt our image, but we are going to prove to the public that this department is crucial to the United States," said Starr in an interview with CNN talk show host Larry King. Starr continued by telling King that his office is questioning the validity of statements made by Attorney General Reno from 1993, when Reno was investigated for the Alcohol, Tobacco, and Firearms involvement in the raid on David Koresh’s compound in Waco, TX.

"I support Reno’s decision in Waco," replied Starr to the feisty King, "but we see many potential misnomers told to the Senate Committee. Most notably when Reno stated that she is in fact a female. We believe this to be a blatant lie."

Starr has hired famous Pittsburgh Medical Examiner Dr. Cyril Wecht to form a team of specialists dedicated to cracking the genetic code of this "man beast."

Reno fired back at these allegations with a vengeance, accusing Starr of libel. However, Reno would not comment on her Deoxyribonucleic Acid (DNA).

In an exclusive interview, Dr. Wecht informed Piggy Lovin’ that he would investigate the possibility of anabolic steroids and illegal growth hormones as well as receded testicles in the body of Reno. Wecht continued to say, "this would explain a great deal about our Attorney General, including [former Surgeon General] Jocelyn Elders’ comments about Reno’s obtrusive member."

Starr is to present the results of his investigation to the Senate Sub-Committee on Gender Typing by October 2000.
