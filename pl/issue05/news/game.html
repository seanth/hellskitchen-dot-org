Computer Game Claims the Lives of Two Children

-Written by Joe Sindoni

The entire town of Provo, Spain is in shock due to the deaths of two children last week. The two children, John Swanson, William Sindoni and a group of friends attempted to play a game called "Capture the Flag", a version of the popular online game QuakeTM. The object of this game is to kill members of the opposing team and capture the other team’s flag before they get yours. This would usually consist of harmless computer fun, but John, William and the rest of the children tried to play a game of Quake in real life, complete with real weapons. The game ended shortly after it began when John and William, who were both on the blue team, died instantly when struck by a rocket from Bill Bal, who had the strength rune and Quad. The other children were shocked, and kept waiting for John and William to "respawn".

Paramedics described the event as "the worst mess they had ever seen", as they picked up the remains piece by piece. Police are still trying to find out how the children got hold of a strength rune and quad due to the ban on the sale of these items to minors. The children purchased the various weapons used (shotguns, rocket and grenade launchers) from the local S-MartTM. (Shop smart, shop S-MartTM ), which is suspect of selling weapons on the black market to groups such as the little known Spanish Mafia. This is all a serious shock to the community of Provo, which is a very small town completely devoid of violence. Bill Bal and the other children involved will be tried for the murder of John and William later next month. When asked about the seriousness of the charges against them, one child stated "I hope we don’t have to serve time, but I heard that the juvenile detention center has Ethernet, so it shouldn’t be all that bad".

This raises many questions about the effects of violence on our children. Should games that contain graphic violence be banned forever? One sociologist, James Humperdink thinks so. "I haven’t seen something this terrible since the Super Mario BrothersTM. Incident in 1988 where a young boy stomped on the head of his brother until he died," stated Humperdink. This event has also caused a great uprising in the Christian community. The local priest of Provo, Father Ramirez-Hidalgo-Sifuentez- Trevino-Iglesias, called this a sign from God. "He is showing how evil can warp the minds of all people," stated Father Ramirez as he watched The Texas Chainsaw MassacreTM. on DVDTM. . Robert Tilton, a well- known televangilist, sent Prayer ClothsTM. and autographed posters to the parents of the deceased. He also asked that they send a pledge of faith of just $25.

Who knows which one of our children could be pushed over the edge next, as video game companies produce even more realistic and violent games? Video games are surely a tool of the Devil and I hope that, with the support of people with similar views, that we can all put an end to violence of any kind.

-A concerned parent, Joe Sindoni
