Ramseys Cut Slack on Rope for HRC Sponsored Pedarest

-Written by Saul Rosenrosen

Boulder, CO-

In a stunning press conference held yesterday, the parents of late child beauty pageant phenom, Jon Benet Ramsey, affirmed rumors that they are donating the rope used to strangle their daughter to the Harry Ransom Center of the University of Texas at Austin.

The rope will be a featured attraction in the HRC’s new Pederast Exhibit. Director Dr. Thomas F. Strapley says the weapon will be draped on a wall against black velvet to show elegance and beauty in an item that aided in sexual molestation and death.

Strapley told Piggy Lovin’ that museum officials want visitors to the HRC to realize that the "death of a future Miss American isn’t so bad."

Staley also informed the Austin Press Corps - consisting of entertainment magazine X.L.ent, college radio station KVRX, the Lanier High School Press, and Piggy Lovin’ that the Pederast Exhibit, entitled "All I Needed To Know I Learned Looking In Kindergartens," will also include the broomstick used to rend and torture Jeffrey Dahmer anally, and John Wayne Gacy’s clown suit.

When asked how the Harry Ransom Center will pay for Kindergarten, University of Texas President, Larry Faulkner, said, "We are toying with many ideas, but so far our best concept is giving patrons the opportunity to strangle a mannequin or small dog with the Ramsey Rope while wearing Gacy’s Clown Suit. We expect lines out the door."

"All I Needed To Know I Learned Looking In Kindergartens" will open to a black tie reception this year on the anniversary of Ramsey’s slaying.
