Don't Fear The Retard

-Written by Saul Rosenrosen

New York City, NY-

Working hard to employ more minorities and people with disabilities in the City of New York, Mayor Rudolph Giulianni announced Tuesday a new addendum to the Metro Transit Authority’s application process for Metro-rail conductor.

The conductor, who runs the subway trains while announcing the rail stops to all passengers, will now be staffed by the most verbally incomprehensible people who apply for a job with the MTA.

An enticing plan on paper, the new program does come with some risk. "The retards. We are worried about the retards," says MTA director E. Virgil Conway. To alleviate fears of placing a cranially defunct person at the helm of subway train, the MTA has automated certain trains so that the "idiots only have to announce the stops."

The automated trains will be slimmed in size, and the City will hold a public hearing about the feasibility of painting these special trains yellow.
