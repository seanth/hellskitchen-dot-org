FAA to SWA: Spend Some Money!

-Written by Saul Rosenrosen

Washington DC-

A new report issued by the Federal Aviation Administration states that Southwest Airlines, "Really fuckin’ sucks." Although priced reasonably lower than other major airlines, Dallas based Southwest is considered "as uncomfortable as a catheter," by 73% of the public, according to a 1998 Piggy Lovin’ survey.

While SWA President Herb Kelleher could not be reached for comment, one FAA employee said to Piggy Lovin’, "A half-ounce bag of pretzels and a Diet Slice: What’s up with that shit? And if I pay $242 to fly out of Manchester, NH, why should I have to sit facing the rear of the plane in a seat that won’t lean back? That shit is whack."

Other complaints by SWA passengers include the "snappyness" of the flight attendants and joy riding pilots who force the Boeing jets into barrel roles which cause the majority of the contusions and concussions frequently given to SWA passengers.

Delta Airlines President and CEO Leo F. Mullin informed Piggy Lovin’ of a projected revenue increase directly related to the FAA report. "It’s good to know that the death trap of the industry ultimately pays for my dinner and my secretary’s lingerie."
