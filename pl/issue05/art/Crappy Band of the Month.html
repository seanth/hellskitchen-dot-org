Crappy Band of the Month

Written By Chi Chi

- Bowling For Soup -



Recently I went to go see Reel Big Fish play at Deep Ellum Live. They put on a pretty good show, but unfortunately, it took nearly a full hour of complete and helpless suffering before I was able to enjoy them. To say the opening band was "horrible" would be an understatement. Bowling For Soup was definitely the worst hour of music I have ever been forced to listen to. They sounded like another Green Day band and had an enormously fat guitar player who could barely walk, let alone play power chords along with the other guitar part - I mean the same power chords that the other guitar player was playing. Which brings me to the other guitar player and lead "singer." This guy's voice was so high, nasal, and whiney that it gave me a headache. It literally hurt to listen to him try to sing. Their music was old and repeatitive, nobody could play more than basic chords and rhythms, and the lead singer's voice caused me physical pain. Bowling For Soup is one of the most painful bands there is to listen to.
