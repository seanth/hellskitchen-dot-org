Undiscovered Band of the Month

Written By Chi Chi

- Brave Combo -

Brave Combo is probably one of the most unique bands around today. Self described as "nuclear polka," they are not afraid to play any type of music, ranging from waltzes, cha-chas, twists, polka, or rock 'n' roll, just to name a few. This group from Denton, TX, has been together for twenty years now and has recorded several great albums along the way. They have received some recognition, including two grammy nominations, but are still not the recognized name they should be. If you live in the D/FW-Denton area, look for them to play at some sort of festival near you. They nearly always do. I recently saw them at the Fort Worth Main Street Arts Festival, which was a great show. The band includes Carl Finch on guitar, accordian, and keyboards, Bubba Hernandez on bass, Jeffery Barnes on sax, clarinet, and reed flute,etc., Joseph Cripps on percussion, Danny O'Brien on trumpet, and Alan Emert on drums. Do not miss an opportunity to see this band. You will never see another band like them.


You can see their website at http://brave.com/bo/
