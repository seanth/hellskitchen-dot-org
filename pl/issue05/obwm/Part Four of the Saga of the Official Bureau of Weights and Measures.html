I was still determined, but without a plan. The world can be a very frustrating place when you don't know how to get the things you know should be yours to begin with. At this point in the story, I was stuck working at an internet coffee shop in Santa Clara, California, sleeping in my car, and showering at the gym. I had come to Santa Clara with a plan that had failed me in more ways that one, and after a few months, I had decided to leave Santa Clara for Washington D.C. completely without any sort of plan at all.

My rationalization for this was simple; nobody ever has a plan in an action movie. They always just bust into a building and kick some ass. I also figured that most of the physicists and engineers working at the center for time would not have lots of guns or anything. I would eventually have to do some preparing, of course, because it was crucial to my plan that nobody in the building escape.

So I drove off one day on a lunch break that I never did come back from, and in a few days I had arrived in Virginia, where I slept in my car and showered at the same gym as in California (well not the same gym but…). I quickly found the center for time, noted the vehicle in the back which could be used for a backup time standard via satellite links, and plotted my course of action.

After much debating, I decided to invest in a small length of rope, a black ninja outfit, and a pair of nunchaku, which I had no idea how to use. Hopefully, I could bust in there, kick just a little ass (I was certain I could beat up a few engineers. How tough can geeky guys be?) and the rest of the workers would submit to me. The point was to look like a ninja master and complete badass.

So I bought a mirror also, and practiced my moves in costume, all the while getting more and more nervous. It is natural to get nervous going into a strange place in a strange outfit, but there was a little more to this. I was excited at the thought of taking over this place, and I was scared of unforeseen problems and unforeseen guards. But none of this scared me out of my plans of seizure. I was ready to scare the crap out of some geeks.
