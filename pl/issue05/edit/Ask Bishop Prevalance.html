Bishop Prevelance answers all of your religous questions


Dear Bishop,
I believe in God and pray everyday for my eternal soul, but I am worried. Why do bad things happen to good people? Does God not care about us, his children?
-Melissa


 -God does care about his children, Melissa, and he cares about you. He just doesn't care about what happens to you.
-Bishop Prevalance




Dear Bishop,
I get the feeling that all of our good christian holidays are turning into purely commercial events. How can God let this happen?
-Kevin


 -Well, God needs an audience, and commercial holidays bring in record crowds at the churches, so if holidays have to be commercialized events in order to bring in the people who would otherwise stay at home and watch football, then hooray for propoganda! This is also why the lord has so many televangelists. If God can come to you instead of making you go to church, that means better ratings for God again (because people are inherently lazy), and hooray for television!
-Bishop Prevalance




Dear Bishop,
Does holy water really hurt vampires, or is that just in movies and books?
-Winnie


 -Well, I think that all depends on the type of holy water you use. If you get the regular priest-blessed water, It may deter a vampire a bit, possibly giving him a rash, but that's about it. The regular grade is really not good for much anything besides baptizing and your usual around-the-church sorts of jobs. If I were you, I would get ahold of some premium grade holy water, that is, water blessed by a higher-ranked employee of God, like a bishop or a cardinal. Premium grade holy water can do everything from baptisms to exorcisms to dishes and even removing those "ungodly" stains in the bathroom. Personally, I have never seen a vampire, but I can pretty much gaurentee you that MY holy water can kill vampires and keep you safe. If you want to buy some, ten ounce bags can be purchased at my church for $14.95 an ounce.
-Bishop Prevalance
