Dear Cha-Chee,
How come I can get my nearsighted vision corrected by laser surgery, but if I shine one of those laser pointers in my eye, I could go blind?
-Jerry


 -Well, you see, the laser fixes your vision by penetrating back into the control centers of your eye, where the calibration settings may be adjusted back to 20/20. This is also why lasers can make you go blind if they get in your eyes. My suggestion to you is that if you do get laser in your eyes, to keep shining it in there (and get it all around) untill your vision is back to normal.
-Cha-Chee
