"Binge Drinking 101"

-By Sir Andrew

The acts of debauchery are not something that most college freshmen are familiar with. College is often times their first serious experience with drinking, and there are many common mistakes made in their novice acts of binge drinking at parties. It follows, then that one who is experienced in this area should offer some advice. That is where I come in.

At most parties you will be forced to drink some sort of carbonated piss-beer. The key to getting over the taste of this beer is to have a chugging contest. After knocking back five beers from your plastic Dixie cup, stop, and converse with those around you. This will allow the beer to start taking effect.

This is the point where most freshmen make their first critical mistake. They think that since they can no longer taste the beer, they should have another chugging contest. Another contest, however, will result in "praying to the porcelain god" for the remainder of the evening. Instead, you should refill your cup and go socialize before your speech becomes too slurred. At this time, the inexperienced drinker will want to go to the bathroom. DO NOT DO THIS! By "breaking the seal" you will subject yourself to going to the bathroom every five minutes until you pass out. Hold it in as long as possible. Yes, this is damaging to your bladder, but you're killing brain cells and destroying your liver already, so why not screw your bladder too?

By beer fifteen, or whenever you lose count of how many you've had, you should be seeing double. This is where the second critical mistake is made.

Most freshmen think that seeing double is bad and that it means they should stop drinking. This is far from the truth. If you stop at this point, you will be sobering up before you can pass out, and that is no good at all. Instead, just remember to pace yourself.

The final stage in drinking is the stage where you are just about to pass out. Most people lie down and try to go to sleep. That is bad. If you do that, the room will spin in a thousand different directions and your beautiful beer will come up in a hurry. First of all, make sure you really are going to pass out by having yet another beer. If you are still standing, lie down, cause you're going to pass out.

WARNING: BEER GOGGLES At about the time you start seeing double, you realize that everyone at the party is remarkably attractive. This observation is generally not true to fact. You now have beer goggles on. BEER GOGGLES ARE NOT YOUR FRIEND! They are evil things that can be more painful than the next morning's hangover. I know this from experience, but luckily, my roommate was there to stop me. Even though he was exceedingly mean to the girl I had found, he was able to wean me away from the evil that could have befallen me.

When you enter the party, simply take a look around, and see if there is anyone you want to hit on. If there is, good. Just remember only to hit on those people you find attractive while you are sober.

Well, that's about it. Follow my advice and you too can be a lush. Oh! Also, if you don't smoke, start. After a week of smoking you will have killed most of the cilia in your throat and severely damaged your taste buds, which in turns leads to easier consumption of bad beer. Have fun!
