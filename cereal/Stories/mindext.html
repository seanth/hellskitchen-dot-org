Knowingly inspired
Stalemated spirits meditate eternity:
"I will always-never tell tales open-laced, Open-visioned,
Eccentric yarns of unfortunate impasse,
Woven, always-never tiled...
Never oblong,
Never encasing extreme desire.

Years open up today,
Open-laced, open-visioned
Eccentric mind extract."
I wonder if language learns patience,
Rationale or temper.
Everything coincides totally.
Yells only understand yells.
One understands reality half-enlightened
After reality tumbles.
"You obviously understand
Needing everything exactly direct...
Mind extract."

Mortality offers regret
Every time he answers "Not yet"
Over-used thoughts heard
Inside narratives kill immortality
Heated umbrage never goes,
Endearing rhetoric fighting over reflex.
"You only understand reflex,
Knowing inside, something's strange."

Behold everything moving inside
Never-ending, naked, outside worlds.
Forget understanding.
Consider knowing mind extract.

No other words.

Mind Extract by James Seti.
