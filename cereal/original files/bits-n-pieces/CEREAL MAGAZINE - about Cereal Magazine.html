Wha= t it is:

Cereal is a free publication out of Rochester, NY with a print circulation of about 1000 approximat= ely once a month. The name is a pun for the word 'serial' which is the type of articles Cereal specializes in; multi-part pieces that can stand by themselves as a story. Cereal also prints poe= try and essays and lovingly illustrates them all.

The Web site contains most of the ar= ticles that have appeared in the latest volumes of the print version of Cereal, as well as a few Internet exclusives.

 

Pur= pose:

To provide subversive literary conte= nt. Stories are allowed to be on taboo subjects and essays present opinions in passioned language that are not to be found normall= y in scholastic publications... which, in a way, this is.<= /b>

And to provide more than just a stag= e for those with enough chutzpah to write such literature, but an entire festival.

The Web site serves to archive past issues and present them to the world.

It also is a showcase of writing and illustrating talent that is looking for steady or freelance wor= k. Contact us if you have any opportunities and like what you see.

 

= History:

Cereal has been in existence since 1= 996 as a part of Hell's Kitchen and Gracie's Dinnertime Theatre as a way to present short stories within their format. It was sporadic in issues - an afterthought. In 1998, it was given to B. Edw= ard Barrett who was looking for something to do at the time.

Since then, Cereal has increased the frequency and regularity of installments, increased the number of stories in each issue, increased its readership following, emboldened its illustrations to new levels, and launched a webs= ite.

 

F= uture:

Cereal is branching out and will be providing more of the insightful essays and brilliant poetry it has started to print, but its main focus will always be on serial fiction - a way it awards especially avid readers.

 

Archives of the Past:

This site begins with Volume 7. Ther= e will be an effort made to archive some of the older volumes of Cereal, but unfortunately many of the issues have been lost to the ages.
