Gracies Dinnertime Theatre has been publishing since 1995 and does so weekly during the academic year by a volunteer staff. It is not a student club. It receives no official funding, support, or encouragement from any university, but it still distributes issues for free because the staff deeply believe in an independent press and that voices should be heard.

Donations are processed through Hell's Kitchen, an organization that GDT helped found to act as a financial umbrella, and to help promote independent voices. Hell's Kitchen is a not-for-profit 501(c)(3) association (EIN: 16-1513436) and has been supporting independent publications since 1998. GDT is simultaneously its oldest member and the reason Hell's Kitchen exists at all. Your contribution is tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law.
