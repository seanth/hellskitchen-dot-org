eggy grumbled as the phone rang before she could open her bottle. "Hello."

"Don't take even the smallest sip from that bottle. If you'll look out your window you'll see why."

Peggy snarled as the stranger hung up then yanked open her curtains to look out on the street. She saw nothing even remotely unusual. Whipping her curtains closed again she turned to retrieve her bottle, nearly running into the man standing behind her.

"See anything, Peggy?"

"How did you get in here?" she stumbled back a step

"Ah, now, but that would be telling. You wouldn't want me to do that, would you?"

"Don't hurt me." She whimpered, backing away another step.

Syzyx looked at her oddly, "I have no desire to hurt you. I just want to help."

"Get away before I call the police."

"They won't help you."

"Why are you here?" she demanded, trying to regain herself.

"I've brought you a gift." He gestured to the bed.

Peggy slowly moved over to the bed, keeping a cautious eye on the albino. A plain white clothing box sat at the foot of the bed.

Carefully she slipped the pearl ribbon off the box and stared at the two jewelry boxes sitting atop the white dress within.

"Syzyx, where di-" she stopped short, confused by the albino's sudden disappearance. She stuffed the box into her closet and reached for her bottle but found it too gone.

"Hello, Peggy. Did you like my gift?"

Peggy stopped on the sidewalk outside school at the sound of Syzyx's voice. Slowly she turned and slapped him, "Go fuck yourself, shithead."

He stood indifferently, touching the corner of his lip with his fingertips, "It wasn't to your liking then."

"What the hell is your problem? Why do you keep bothering me?" "I like you. And I don't like to see the people I like hurt themselves."

"Oh, so you're not an evil assassin anymore? Now you're my guardian fucking angel?"

"I never said I was an evil assassin. Or your guardian angel."

"Lies? I knew it."

"Some. But I do like you."

Peggy muttered then punched the albino full in the face, sprawling him on the ground, "Leave me the fuck alone. Don't come to my fucking house. And don't touch my fucking bottle!"

"I can't do that." Syzyx said, sitting up.

Peggy growled and kicked him in the chest, knocking him down again, "Do I have to beat the shit out of you or do you get it that I don't want to have anything to do with you?"

Syzyx stood up slowly and watched her for a calm moment, noticing for the first time the blood trickling down his face. He nodded to her and turned away.

eggy walked back the way she was headed, picking up her pace as three men began following her. She reached the top of the hill when two of the three grabbed her. She tried to scream but a hand was quickly clamped over her mouth. The three dragged her into the small patch of woods beside the road.

"Don't worry, sweet stuff. We ain't gonna hurt you. If you cooperate." The apparent leader smiled, beginning to unbuckle his belt.

Peggy tried futilely to scream as a second of the trio tore away her skirt.

"Just lie still and you won't get hurt." The leader growled as Peggy kicked out at him.

"Gentlemen, I suggest you refrain." A soft voice warned.

The gang leader looked back at the albino standing at the edge of the woods and motioned for the third of the trio to deal with him.

"You just found yourself a little trouble pale assed freak," The henchman sneered, "I know karate."

Syzyx stood calmly, "I hope you know it well enough to save you if he goes any further."

The henchman snarled and kicked out at the albino's knee. Syzyx took a single step backward to avoid the kick, taking the moment his assailant needed to recover to draw a pistol from his boot. He leveled the gun at his attacker and fired once. The soft sound of escaping air was followed by the henchman's kneecap exploding. The second henchman spun at his comrades scream with a pistol of his own.

"Beat this, shithead." The henchman barked, pulling the trigger.

Syzyx stood his ground, only wincing slightly after each of the three shots hit him. He took a moment to aim then shot the man in the throat.

"You made a mistake." Syzyx placed the gun against the back of the leaders head, "Get off of her."

"That makes me angry, pu-" the gang leader stopped, staring wide eyed at the gun barrel in his face, "Come on man, no need for that. We were just having some fun, you understand. Right?"

"Get out of here. Your friend will need a good surgeon for his knee." The two survivors scrambled away into the woods, neither looking back. "Are you all right?" Syzyx asked, handing Peggy her skirt without looking at her.

"Oh, yeah, fine. I've just been witness to a murder and you want to know if I'm fucking all right? I could've handled it myself."

"Of course. I'm sorry." The albino said softly, unbuttoning his shredded shirt.

"What the hell are you doing? Shouldn't you get to a hospital with that?"

He looked at her oddly for a moment before answering, "No. The blood isn't real. This vest was designed by the military for special operations personnel if they were ever found and shot. The pockets of blood would make it look like a hit had been scored and the agent could escape after the enemy had left. I am going to be bruised however."

"Military? Where'd you get it, then?"

"Remember how I told you my father died in Panama? He forgot his vest."

"He gave it to you? Why?"

Syzyx stretched his arm and turned away from Peggy, rubbing his shoulder, "Being the son of a known hitman isn't a safe position. Especially with my fathers record of kills."

"How many does he have?"

"Far too many by my count. He was good."

"Where do you live?"

"If you want me, I'll be there."
