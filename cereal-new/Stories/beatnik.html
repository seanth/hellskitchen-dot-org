o I sat down to write an article with no boundaries and was reminded of that Buddhist proverb- "When all doors are open, all doors are closed." In other words, "You mean I can do whatever I want? I'll be sure to be creatively stumped for at least three months." Then it occurred to me that I should just do like the masters say and write what I know and what I am interested in. "Well that's easy," I thought, I'm completely obsessed with my own life, so I'll just write about that. Hence, this series was born.

Today's self-absorbed topic will be My Reaction to My Film Studies Class Today.

My teacher was introducing Chaplin to us as a powerful figure in film evolution. He gave us notes about how Chaplin starred in, produced, directed and wrote the screenplay for The Kid. Then he asked us: "Now who can tell me another word for 'screenplay?'" He said this in his usual manner which I find difficult to differentiate between condescending, encouraging, and giddy. The room was silent.

"Okay," he said, beginning to motion over the classroom, "I'll ask this side of the room." Slowly, he worked his way over some very interesting responses -

"Scenery?"

"Uh . . .Lighting!"

"Choreog . . .choreo . .um . .kerg-"

"Acting?"

"Screenplay."

"Gaffer?"

"You said it wasn't screenplay?"

Now, how in a media-phile society like ours, where a scientologist can be offered more money for a single film that all the public schools in Upstate New York will probably receive in federal funding in an entire year can an entire senior Film Studies class in an upper-class suburb not know what the word "screenplay" means?

Perhaps the aforementioned scenario has reaped greater repercussions than previously realized...

I mean, I really hate feeling that my peers are stupid, but it just gives dirty old guys around town another cheesy pick-up line: "Wow, Marisa, you know, you are really mature for your age."

When a girl who collects Hello Kitty paraphernalia and uses "Ozzy rocks!" as a general statement of excitement and glee is considered mature for her age, there is definitely something wrong with people her age.

You know, it can't even be called "retardation," because that would imply slowness and these people are average in their ways.

It's a sad day when you come to realize that the average adult is dumber than you and the average kid my age is even dumber than that (but not by much).

It seems society is filled with this enormous messentary layer of people who cannot even comprehend doing anything half-way intelligent or creative with their lives. They are sandwich between "special" people and the "honors" people. Most of these "honors" people, though intelligent, are uncreative, over-diligent freaks who all want to head for whatever profession they feel will result in the largest possible bank account.

It's like Brave New World in many ways. A huge, mass group of consumers and producers in an endless cycle being watched over by a few individuals who try to keep things running smoothly. Knowledge for the sake of itself, with no worth but its own, becomes less of a reality every day as I walk into school and am bludgeoned over the head with just how ignorant and/or dull people can be when they really put their minds to it.

I guess that's why creative, moody people are so lonely, why so many are self-destructive. The world doesn't need them to exist. They are the unneeded salt for flavor, and probably just as well left in the cabinet. Their disgust at society more than likely stemming from resentment that society has not allowed them their own comfortable slot within its gearwork.

Uncreative stupidity or just social adaptation?

When Friday night rolls around and I'm re-reading newspaper articles about killer sperm while my film classmates get drunk and party after the football game, I'll clue everyone in on what I think is the answer.
