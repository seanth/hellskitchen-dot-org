LETTER FROM THE EDITOR

First off, thanks for tuning in.

If you've been here before, you'll notice a few improvements. The layout is simplified. The graphics load an average of 59% faster. The guestbook is now up and running, so please sign in. In order to get notification of updates, you do not have to sign up with Onelist anymore, but just need to fill out a simple form, so your E-mail address stays with me, and only me, to the grave.

If you haven't been here before, let me warn you that some, but not all, of these stories are a little racy; with vulgarity, inappropriate behavior, adult situations and even a little soft eroticism. If there were such a rating, this site would probably be a PG-13.

- BEB
