    • What it is:

    Cereal is a free publication out of Rochester, NY. The name is a pun for the word 'serial' which is the type of articles Cereal specializes in: multi-part pieces that can stand by themselves as a story. Cereal also prints poetry and essays and lovingly illustrates them all.

     

    Purpose:

    To provide subversive, long-form literary content. Stories are allowed to be on taboo subjects and essays present opinions in passioned language that are not to be found normally in scholastic publications... which, in a way, this is.

    And to provide more than just a stage for those with enough chutzpah to write such literature, but an entire festival.

    The Web site serves to archive past issues and present them to the world.

    It also is a showcase of writing and illustrating talent that is looking for steady or freelance work. Contact us if you have any opportunities and like what you see.

     

    History:

    Cereal has been in existence since 1996 as a part of Hell's Kitchen and Gracies Dinnertime Theatre as a way to present short stories within their format. It was sporadic in issues - an afterthought. In 1998, it was given to B. Edward Barrett who was looking for something to do at the time.

    Under Brian's steady hand, Cereal increased the frequency and regularity of installments, increased the number of stories in each issue, increased its readership following, emboldened its illustrations to new levels, and launched a website (you are here).
